Why Students Need an AI Humanizer

If you're using AI tools like ChatGPT to help brainstorm or draft your essays, you've probably noticed a problem: the writing sounds stiff, repetitive, and uses overly complex words like "furthermore" or "tapestry."

Our AI Humanizer helps you take those rough AI drafts and rewrite them to sound like a real student wrote them.

How to Humanize Your Essays

Follow these steps to get the best results for academic work:

  • Set the Tone to "Academic": This ensures the vocabulary remains formal without sounding like a robot.
  • Use the Voice Profile: Paste a previous essay you wrote yourself into the Voice Profile tool. The AI will learn your sentence structure and apply it to the new text.
  • Keep it Balanced: Use "Strength 2" for the best mix of human-like flow and academic rigor.
