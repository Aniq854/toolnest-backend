The Problem with AI Content in SEO

Google's Helpful Content Update focuses heavily on user experience. If your blog posts are clearly generated by ChatGPT, users will bounce, reducing your dwell time and ultimately tanking your rankings. AI writing often lacks emotion, uses repetitive transitional phrases, and fails to connect with the reader.

How to Humanize SEO Content

Use our AI Humanizer to inject life into your drafts:

  • Select "Blog" Tone: This setting removes overly formal AI words and replaces them with conversational, engaging language.
  • Improve Readability: The tool automatically increases your Flesch Reading Ease score, making your content easier to skim and digest.
  • Maintain Formatting: The humanizer keeps your HTML tags and Markdown intact so you don't have to reformat your blog post.
